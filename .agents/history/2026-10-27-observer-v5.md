# AutoMix observer v5 repair

Implemented evaluator corrections without changing planner, analysis, audio callback
or playback behavior. `observer_version` is 5; `ORDER_VERSION` stays 15. Existing
uncommitted production work was preserved. No commit, push, install or publication.

## Changes

- Invert the same source clock used for observation, including incoming launch,
  variable rates, explicit/implicit curves, curve extension and recovery. Phrase
  exposure is in wall seconds and uses preceding incoming controls where relevant.
- Unsupported Loop/scratch/insert/send FX and unavailable frames report unknown
  coverage. No fabricated zero-overlap or complete listening acceptance.
- Separate nominal stop, observed heard endpoint, active source tail, overlap
  runs, gap-tolerant continuity and lower/upper observation bounds. Keep the old
  8%-or-8-second / four-bar screen explicitly labeled as a legacy descriptor.
- Retire the uncalibrated integrity score. Report instrumental absence, vocal
  carry, missing foreground, held-note release and vocal phrase risk separately.
- Preserve per-occurrence and per-grouping risks in comparisons. Terminal counts
  and instrumental absence alone are not deterioration gates. Reject cross-version
  comparisons and mismatched occurrence coverage. Explicit cycles exclude extra
  context edges and distinguish repeated playlist entries of the same track.

## Reassessment

Fresh read-only library backup and full BLAKE3 verification matched all 76 audio
files, analysis v26 payloads and playlist occurrences to the frozen v15 plans.
Re-evaluated three cycles for BEST and fixed ORDER. BEST ordering itself was not
rerun, intentionally isolating observer changes on identical playback plans.

- BEST legacy-screen candidates: 12 in the first cycle, 13 in each later cycle.
  By playlist after wraparound: Melodic 0, Electronic 3, MUG 8, DnB 2.
- Fixed ORDER: 34 in the first cycle, 33 later. First-pass BEST without wraparound
  is 10 candidates in 72 internal adjacencies.
- Four BEST pairs per cycle have unavailable DSP observation (three loops and
  one FX send). They are not four additional confirmed terminal cases.
- Eroge → Fire Away's 0.45 s bed absence is voice-carried. Still Here → Dreams
  and Don't you want me → Ember retain 1.10 s and 3.50 s foreground-gap risks.
- Luolimasi's last observed sound is about 11.91 s before file EOF, but only
  2.76 s before the active signal end. File-relative timing alone cannot determine
  whether this case is a false tail flag or a musically justified exit.
- In the separately frozen reported chain, Lost & Found → Feel This Good has
  a held-instrument-note risk and 3.80 s foreground gap; We Are The Energy →
  In The Echo has an 8.30 s foreground gap; In The Echo → Underwater retains
  an abrupt tonal-loss flag. Default vocal detection zeros do not certify lyrics.

All thirteen warm-cycle candidates have numeric evidence, a provisional assessment
and a real stem-render link in the local report. No row received new human listening
acceptance. Existing sets remain regression material, not a holdout; thresholds
are explicit screening assumptions awaiting independent listening calibration.
